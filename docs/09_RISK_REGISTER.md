## Highest-exposure cluster
Under the **macOS-first** decision (2026-06), the Windows-specific risks (R6, R8, R15, and the severe half of R1) are **deferred** — revival reference, not active work. The defining risks of *this* rebuild are now **R2–R4 plus R13/R14**: the **XDJ-XZ MIDI reality**, **no hardware yet**, **no Josh**, and whether the **show actually looks good** and **transitions feel right**. None block the demo-first software work; all become acute at the hardware-integration boundary. Plan so that **everything not gated on hardware is finished and tested before hardware access**, so the limited/uncertain hardware window is spent only on genuine hardware unknowns.
